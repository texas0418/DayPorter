Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

DayPorter is a CRM for commercial cleaning and janitorial businesses. It is built to be local-first: the information you enter lives on your device, not on our servers. This policy explains exactly what that means and the one narrow exception (processing your in-app purchase).

The short version: Your clients, sites, contracts, bids, notes, and activity never leave your phone. We don't run a server, and we can't see your data. We don't use analytics, ads, or trackers.

What we store, and where

  • Your CRM data (clients, sites, contracts, bids, follow-ups, notes, and your bid-calculator defaults) is stored only in a private database on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else.
  • Backups are plain files you choose to export. They are created and shared entirely under your control; we never receive them.
  • Uninstalling DayPorter deletes this on-device data.

In-app purchases

DayPorter offers a one-time "Pro" unlock. Purchases are handled by Apple's App Store. To validate the purchase and remember that you unlocked Pro, we use RevenueCat. For this purpose only, RevenueCat receives:

  • an anonymous app-generated identifier (not your name, email, or Apple ID), and
  • your purchase history for this app (whether Pro was bought or restored).

This data is used solely to deliver your Pro entitlement. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for advertising or tracking. Apple's handling of the transaction is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync.

Children

DayPorter is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the new version will be posted on this page.
