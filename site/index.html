Why a CRM for just this

Most commercial cleaning companies run their book of business out of a phone's notes app, a whiteboard, and one spreadsheet nobody else can open. The generic CRMs that would replace it are built for software sales teams: they want deal values and close probabilities and quarterly quotas, and they have no idea what a production rate is.

The janitorial business has a shape of its own. Revenue is recurring, so the number that matters is monthly, not per-deal. Pricing comes from square footage and labor hours, not from what the customer will bear. A client is really a set of buildings, each with its own scope. And the whole thing turns on a walkthrough — a stage that exists in no other industry's pipeline.

DayPorter is that shape, and only that shape. It will never ask you what quarter you expect to close in.

Everything is local. There is no account to create and no server holding your customer list or your margins — a competitor cannot subpoena, breach, or buy what was never uploaded. Export a versioned backup whenever you want.

DayPorter is free for your first 10 clients; one purchase unlocks unlimited clients forever. Sites, contracts, bids, follow-ups and export stay free either way — there is no subscription.

Common questions

Is DayPorter available yet?

It is in App Store review now. The bid calculator on this site is free and works today, and the app will be listed here the moment it is approved.

What does it cost?

Free for your first 10 clients. A single one-time purchase unlocks unlimited clients — no subscription, ever. Sites, contracts, bids, follow-up reminders and export are free no matter what.

How does the bid calculator work?

Cleanable square footage divided by your production rate gives labor hours per visit; visits per month annualise across 52 weeks; hours times your loaded labor rate gives labor cost; supplies ride on top as a percentage; and margin is taken as a share of price. You can try the whole thing free right here.

Can I track more than one building for a client?

Yes — that is the point. Clients hold sites, sites hold contracts. A property manager with six buildings on three different schedules is one client, not six.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. It is a universal app, because walkthroughs and bid reviews often happen on a tablet in the field.

Where is my data kept?

On your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking. Keep device backups switched on so your history survives a lost phone, and export a JSON backup any time you want a copy you control.
